This diagram illustrates the path of an Ethernet frame as it travels from a source computer through a switch to a destination computer, alongside the structure and fields that make up an Ethernet frame. The flow starts at PC A, which acts as the source, sending data packaged into an Ethernet frame. The frame moves to a switch, specifically noted as connected to port 3, which forwards the frame onward to PC B, acting as the destination. Below this path, the diagram breaks down the internal components of an Ethernet frame into its specific sequential fields and their corresponding sizes.
